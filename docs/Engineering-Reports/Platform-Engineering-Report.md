## Status convention

- **Standing** — retained as part of the later platform baseline.
- **Superseded** — explicitly replaced by a later design.
- **Validated** — selected after analysis or physical/software testing.
- **Requirement** — documented design/verification requirement; implementation may be covered separately.

## Phase narrative

The register now captures the major platform transitions from the early cargo/structure and communications baseline, through PT2’s measurable flight/electrical/payload requirements, PT3’s distributed avionics and redundant sensing architecture, and the Dev-Kit’s test-driven production refinements. The final M1 pass will continue expanding this register toward the task target of roughly 60–100 decisions and will normalize source anchors and replacement chains after upstream M0 approval.
