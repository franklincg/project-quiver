## Status convention

- **Standing** — retained as part of the later platform baseline.
- **Superseded** — explicitly replaced by a later design.
- **Validated** — selected after analysis or physical/software testing.
- **Requirement** — documented design/verification requirement; implementation may be covered separately.
- **Open** — an explicitly unresolved decision, integration gap, source disagreement, or verification item.

## Phase narrative

The register now captures the major platform transitions from the early cargo/structure and communications baseline, through PT2’s measurable flight/electrical/payload requirements, PT3’s distributed avionics and redundant sensing architecture, and the Dev-Kit’s test-driven production refinements. The 2026 pass now adds current firmware, propulsion, networking, obstacle-avoidance, and unresolved RF/mechanical integration decisions while preserving source disagreements instead of normalizing them away. The final M1 pass will continue toward the task target of roughly 60–100 decisions, tighten anchors, and close replacement chains after upstream M0 approval.
